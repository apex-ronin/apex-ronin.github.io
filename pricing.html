Frequently asked, honestly answered.

Is this a legal compliance guarantee?

No. We report what the public state registries show at the timestamp of the read. Final contracting decisions and legal verification remain yours.

How fresh is the data?

As fresh as the underlying state board publication. We record the exact UTC timestamp on every snapshot so you never confuse our read time with a board's internal processing lag.

Do my subs need to do anything?

No. Zero portal logins, zero vendor questionnaires, zero onboarding friction for your subcontractors.

What if you find nothing?

You get a clean coverage ledger showing every entity checked, its active licence and bond dates, and the timestamped proof that you checked.
