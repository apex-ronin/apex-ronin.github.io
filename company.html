Operating principles

01Public record before private access. If a risk can be detected from public registries or public storefronts, we do not ask for credentials or portal rollouts.
02Deterministic before generative. Fixed procedures fetch, hash, and diff the record. Models interpret and rank only after the evidence is stored.
03Publish the boundary. Coverage gaps, registry update lag, and what a report does not guarantee are written on the report itself.
04Narrow products, shared substrate. Each commercial product solves one expensive operational blind spot on top of a common evidence engine.
05No borrowed credibility. No invented logos, no fabricated case studies, and no pretending a roadmap stage is already in general release.
