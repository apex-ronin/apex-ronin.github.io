Section 04 / Evidence standard

Every finding carries its source. Every absence is stated as absence.

Deterministic collection establishes the record. Interpretation is layered on top and explicitly marked.

Deterministic first

Registry reads, diffs, and timestamps are captured by fixed procedures before any model touches them.

Cited, not asserted

Every row in a report names the state board, the licence key, and the UTC timestamp of the read.

Human in the loop

Ambiguous entity matches and status transitions are reviewed before an exception alert is sent.

Stated limits

Where a board does not publish a field or lags in updates, the report states that boundary plainly.
