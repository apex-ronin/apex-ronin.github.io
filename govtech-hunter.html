Built to be examined.

Engineering choices documented in the public repository.

01

Local-first inference

Configurable local and API-based model routing lets the deployment choose its inference path. External providers need their own credentials and connectivity.

02

Grounded draft outputs

The drafting pipeline retrieves corpus material and checks cited references against retrieved clause identifiers before classifying an output.

03

Operational visibility

The Observatory records pipeline runs and surfaces source counts, errors, and the model provider used for analysis.
